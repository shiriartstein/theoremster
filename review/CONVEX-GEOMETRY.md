“Mahler’s theorem” is interpreted as the planar volume-product theorem. The
card does not assert a current status for the higher-dimensional Mahler
conjectures. The 1938/1939 bibliographic discrepancy is visible on the card.

The isoperimetric card is the planar inequality, dated to Hurwitz’s 1902
Fourier-method proof, not to the origin or first solution of the problem.
Slicing dates the 2025 journal publication; its 2024 preprint is noted.
Godbersen dates the July 2026 proof preprint by Kotrbatý and Mouamine, with
an October equality-extension note. Neither is presented as a journal article.
The Santaló front says ellipsoids attain equality; the later general only-if
characterization is explained in the notes.

Convex bodies are compact convex sets with nonempty interior. Mixed volume
uses the normalization V(K[n]) = |K|. Polar and Santaló-point notation is
explained on the relevant card backs. Historical sources and aliases are
in the workbook; the JSON here is an audit snapshot, not a second source.

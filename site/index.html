How to play

  1. Everyone starts with one theorem in their timeline, showing its year, and a few ∎ tokens (2 unless changed at setup). The goal is to collect a set number of cards first.
  2. On your turn, read the new theorem and tap the gap in your timeline where you think it belongs. Equal years count as correct on either side.
  3. If you know who proved it, name them before the card is revealed: out loud on a shared screen, or typed in when playing online (small spelling mistakes are fine). A correct name earns you a ∎ (up to 5). On cards with several provers, any one of them counts. Unless the game was set up otherwise, the name only counts if you also placed the card correctly.
  4. Before the reveal, other players may challenge: spend one ∎ and pick a different gap in your timeline. Each gap can hold only one challenge.
  5. Reveal the card. If you placed it right, you keep it. If you didn’t, a challenger who picked the right gap takes it into their own timeline. Otherwise it is discarded.
  6. At the start of a turn, any player can trade 3 ∎ for a card from the deck, placed correctly in their timeline.
  7. The first player to reach the target wins. If the deck runs out, the most cards wins, then the most ∎.
